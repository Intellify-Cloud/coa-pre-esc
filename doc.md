## 1. Brand Positioning

**Brand Name:** Coastal Preferred Escapes (CPE)

**Tagline:**
**Your Beach. Your Family. Your Escape.**

**Positioning Statement:**
CPE is a flexible, membership-based beach holiday club designed for South African families who want affordable annual holidays without the rigid rules and lifetime obligations traditionally associated with timeshare.

Cabana Mio is the flagship resort featured through CPE.

---

## 2. Launch Inventory Strategy

### Market Initially

* **Value Escape Weeks**
* **Preferred Escape Weeks**

### Do NOT Market Publicly Yet

* Peak holiday weeks
* Christmas / New Year
* Easter peak inventory
* Premium school holiday inventory

Peak weeks remain available for direct rental, upgrades, owner use, and future premium membership products.

---

## 3. Membership Structure (In Principle)

### CPE Value Escape

* Joining Fee: **R2,500**
* Monthly Contribution: **R750**
* Flexible booking within the **Value season pool**
* Sleeps up to **6 guests**
* **Free unlimited Wi-Fi included**

### CPE Preferred Escape

* Joining Fee: **R3,500**
* Monthly Contribution: **R1,050**
* Flexible booking within the **Preferred season pool**
* Sleeps up to **6 guests**
* **Free unlimited Wi-Fi included**

---

## 4. The Holiday Wallet System

Members accumulate holiday value through monthly contributions.

### Core Rule

> **A holiday may only be confirmed when sufficient Holiday Wallet value is available, or when the shortfall has been paid in full before arrival.**

This is a savings-based holiday membership, not a financed instalment sale.

---

## 5. Membership Pause Protection

### One pause of up to 6 months every 5 years

* Wallet balance remains protected
* No monthly contributions during the pause
* Booking rights temporarily suspended
* Membership may be resumed at any time by restarting contributions

This feature is intended to create goodwill and long-term member retention.

## 11. Launch Objective

The purpose of the first 12 months is **not to sell all available inventory**.

The objective is to:

* Establish the **CPE brand**
* Build **recurring monthly income**
