# Coding Agent Brief — Cabana Mio Winter Sun Single Page

Build a polished, responsive single-page marketing website for a new long-stay winter escape offer at Cabana Mio in Amanzimtoti, South Africa.

## Core Goal

The page must sell a simple idea:

**Spend 60–90 days escaping the European winter in a spacious 3-bedroom beachside duplex in Amanzimtoti, South Africa.**

The primary audience is retired or semi-retired visitors from:

* United Kingdom
* Netherlands
* Germany
* Sweden
* Norway
* Denmark

The strongest audience profile is:

* Age 55+
* Couples
* Golfers
* Retired or semi-retired
* Looking for winter sun
* Wanting better value than Spain or Portugal
* Wanting enough space for visiting family

The site should feel calm, premium, trustworthy and aspirational without looking like an expensive luxury-resort website.

---

# Positioning

Do not present this as normal holiday accommodation.

Present it as:

**Your winter home by the Indian Ocean.**

Key selling points:

* 60–90 day long stays
* 3-bedroom duplex
* Around 50 metres from the beach
* Warm subtropical climate
* Golf courses nearby
* Space for visiting family
* Unlimited Wi-Fi
* Optional housekeeping
* Airport transfer assistance
* English-speaking destination
* Much more space than a typical European winter rental

# 11. FAQ

Use an accessible accordion.

Suggested questions:

### How long can I stay?

The main package is designed around 90-night stays, with some 60-day stays potentially available.

### How many people can stay?

The duplex has three bedrooms. Package pricing should clearly state included occupancy.

### Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes, unlimited Wi-Fi should be positioned as included if operationally correct.

### How close is the beach?

Cabana Mio is approximately 50 metres from the beach.

### Can family visit during our stay?

Yes, subject to the unit's normal occupancy limits.

### Can you arrange airport transfers?

Transfers can be arranged on request.

### Are golf courses nearby?

Yes. Amanzimtoti and the South Coast provide access to several golf courses.

### How do payments work?

Use a deposit-based booking structure. Do not promote monthly instalment plans.

### What currency can I pay in?

Position EUR and GBP prominently, with final settlement handled through the chosen payment provider.

# Design Direction

The visual style should feel:

* coastal
* calm
* warm
* slightly premium
* mature
* trustworthy
* spacious

Avoid:

* overly corporate layouts
* excessive rounded cards
* glassmorphism
* neon gradients
* generic SaaS UI
* massive amounts of animation
* cluttered sections
* stock-photo-heavy presentation

Use strong photography and typography rather than decoration.

Suggested visual palette:

* deep ocean/navy
* turquoise / teal
* warm sand / off-white
* subtle coral or sunset accent

Maintain high contrast and accessibility.

---

# Typography

Use a modern, highly readable typeface.

Suitable options:

* Manrope
* Inter
* DM Sans
* Source Sans 3

Headings can have slightly more personality but should remain easy to read for an older audience.

Do not use tiny body text.

Minimum body copy should generally be around 17–18px on desktop.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Build mobile-first.

Suggested breakpoints:

* 0–559px: mobile
* 560–767px: large mobile / small tablet
* 768–1199px: tablet / small desktop
* 1200px+: desktop

Use responsive layouts fluidly rather than designing entirely separate versions.

On mobile:

* hero text should remain readable
* CTAs stack
* pricing remains prominent
* navigation collapses
* avoid overly tall empty sections
* imagery should crop intelligently

---

# Performance

Prioritise performance.

Requirements:

* responsive images
* WebP or AVIF where appropriate
* lazy load below-the-fold images
* minimise JavaScript
* no unnecessary frameworks or libraries
* avoid heavy animation libraries
* optimise Core Web Vitals

# Technical Quality

Code must be:

* clean
* maintainable
* semantic
* accessible
* componentised where appropriate
* free from unnecessary duplication

Use BEM-style CSS/SCSS naming if the project uses SCSS.

Centralise:

* colours
* spacing
* typography
* breakpoints
* border radii
* shadows

Do not hard-code design values repeatedly throughout components.

---

# Accessibility

Meet sensible WCAG AA standards.

Ensure:

* proper heading hierarchy
* keyboard navigation
* visible focus states
* sufficient colour contrast
* form labels
* accessible accordion behaviour
* alt text
* semantic landmarks
* minimum comfortable tap targets

---

# Important Content Rule

The page must not make claims that cannot be verified.

Do not invent:

* exact golf-course distances
* airport travel times
* guaranteed temperatures
* included services
* ratings
* testimonials
* availability
* payment providers

Where facts have not yet been confirmed, use a clear placeholder or write copy that does not require the claim.

---

# Final Outcome

The finished page should make a visitor understand within approximately 10 seconds:

1. This is in South Africa.
2. It is aimed at people escaping the European winter.
3. They can stay for around 60–90 days.
4. They get a spacious 3-bedroom beachside duplex.
5. Golf and the beach are major lifestyle benefits.
6. The 90-day package costs around €7,950 / £6,850.
7. They can enquire about availability immediately.

The primary conversion goal is:

**Generate a qualified enquiry for a 60–90 day winter stay.**
